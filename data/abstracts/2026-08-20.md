Michael Bauer, a software architect and software engineer with 15+ years of experience, takes us on a **journey through type-systems from their origins in mathematics & philosophy** to the cutting edge of programming languages today and beyond, showing us why their usefulness extends far above variable declarations.

- Chart the deep connections between logic, math, and software
- Build increasingly expressive abstractions
- Turn bookkeeping into powerful proofs about your code
- Provide high-value signals for both humans and AI
- Write less code and fewer tests with better guarantees
