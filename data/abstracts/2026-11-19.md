Felix Dorner is a software developer from Düsseldorf who works mainly with Java and JVM technologies. Like many of us, he’s spent years **in the world of classes**, frameworks and mutable state, and he’s got curious what happens when you keep the platform but **change the programming paradigm**.

This talk is an **introduction to Clojure**, a modern Lisp that runs on the JVM and gives you full access to the Java ecosystem you already know.
