We’re excited to kick off our first Java Meetup of the year with a real highlight: **Maven 4!**

Maven has been with us for over 20 years and now the next big step is just around the corner.

Matthias Bünger is a **Maven committer** and **maintainer of the JUnit extension library _JUnit Pioneer_**.

In his talk he speaks about the **motivation and challenges the team had while developing Maven 4** and, of course, about its **new features and improvements**.

Matthias loves Java, Jakarta EE, testing and documentation.
